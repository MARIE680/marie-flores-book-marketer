Amazon Discovery Strategy

Help the right readers find your book when they’re already searching.

What’s Included

A full audit of your current Amazon listing and keyword coverage
Category and subcategory recommendations based on real competitive research
A revised book description built around how readers actually search
KDP advertising campaigns structured around proven discovery opportunities
Ongoing bid and keyword optimization as results come in
A clear report on what changed and why

Who It’s For

Authors who have a published book on Amazon but feel invisible next to comparable titles, and authors preparing for launch who want their listing built correctly from day one.

The Process

01

Audit

We start by reviewing your current listing, keywords, categories, and reviews against your genre and competitors.

02

Strategy

We map out the keywords, categories, and positioning changes with the strongest discovery opportunity.

03

Optimize

We implement the changes to your listing, backend keywords, and category placement.

04

Advertise

We set up and launch KDP advertising campaigns aligned to your new positioning.

05

Refine

We monitor performance and adjust keywords, bids, and targeting on an ongoing basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already be running ads?
No. Many authors start with the audit and listing work alone, and add advertising once the foundation is solid.
How long before I see movement in rankings?
Most authors see measurable keyword and category movement within four to eight weeks, though timelines vary by genre and competition.
Will this work for a backlist title, not just a new release?
Yes. Backlist titles often have the most room for improvement, since the original listing was rarely built with search behavior in mind.
