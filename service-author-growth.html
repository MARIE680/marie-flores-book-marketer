Author Growth Strategy

Build a marketing system around your whole catalog, not just one book.

What’s Included

A strategy for how each book in a series supports the others
A backlist marketing plan for older titles that still have discovery potential
Author positioning that clarifies who you write for and why
Cross-promotion opportunities with comparable authors and titles
Ongoing marketing support across your full catalog

Who It’s For

Authors with more than one published book, or authors planning a series, who want a long-term marketing system instead of restarting from zero with every release.

Frequently Asked Questions

I only have one book out. Is this still relevant?
This service is most valuable once you have two or more books, or a series in progress. A single-title author is usually better served by Amazon Discovery or Book Launch first.
Is this a long-term commitment?
It is designed as an ongoing relationship, though scope and cadence are set together based on your goals.
Can this include a book still in progress?
Yes. Positioning and series strategy are often strongest when planned before the next book is finished.
