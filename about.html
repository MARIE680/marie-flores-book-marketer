About Marie

Strategic Marketing for Authors Who Want More Than Just Visibility.

A career built on one belief: that a book’s success is decided less by how it is promoted and more by whether the right readers can find it in the first place.

Who Marie Is

Marie Flores has spent more than eight years working directly with authors on Amazon, helping books become visible to the audiences most likely to read and love them. That work now spans more than 267 authors and 400 books across fiction and nonfiction, in formats ranging from debut novels to established series and long-running nonfiction backlists.

A book’s success is decided less by how loudly it is promoted, and more by whether the right readers can actually find it.

That distinction shapes every project. Rather than opening with a generic promotional package, work begins with an honest look at where a book currently stands, what readers are searching for, and where the real opportunity lies.

Philosophy and Approach

Marketing that starts with promotion before understanding tends to waste an author’s time and budget on channels that were never a good fit. Marie’s approach reverses that order: audit first, strategy second, and only then optimization and promotion, so that every dollar spent is working toward a documented opportunity rather than a guess.

This is why discovery comes before promotion in every engagement. A perfectly executed advertising campaign pointed at the wrong keywords, the wrong categories, or the wrong readers will still underperform. Getting the foundation right first is what makes everything built on top of it work harder.
